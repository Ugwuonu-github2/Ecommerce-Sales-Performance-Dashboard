# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze the sales performance of an e-commerce business using the Online Retail II dataset. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Power Query, and interactive slicers.

The objective is to demonstrate how Excel can be used as a Business Intelligence tool for executive reporting and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Business managers need quick answers to questions such as:

- How much revenue has the business generated?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which countries contribute the most revenue?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which weekdays generate the most sales?

This dashboard provides answers to these questions through an interactive reporting interface.

## 💡 Business Insights

- Generated over **$9.7 million** in total revenue.
- The United Kingdom accounted for the largest share of sales.
- Sales showed clear seasonal patterns, with peak revenue occurring toward the end of the year.
- A small number of products generated a significant proportion of total revenue.
- Interactive filtering allows users to explore sales performance by year, month, country, and weekday.
